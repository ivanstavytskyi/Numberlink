Frequently asked questions answered
How do I start a game?
Select your preferred board size in the settings panel, then press the PLAY button to begin.
Can I play on different devices?
Yes, NumberLink supports cross‑device play. Your progress is saved automatically.
Are there difficulty levels?
Difficulty scales with board size. Larger grids provide more complex puzzles.
How is scoring calculated?
Your score is based on the formula: 10,000 ÷ completion time (in seconds). Faster solutions earn higher points.
Can I submit feedback?
Absolutely! Use the Player Reviews section to rate the game and share your experience.
